## Recipe

- 600 g cherry tomatoes
- 200 g feta
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 4 cloves garlic, peeled
- 0.5 tsp red chili flakes
- 350 g penne
- 100 g spinach
- 15 g basil
- 1 pinch black pepper

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
2. Tip the tomatoes and garlic into a baking dish and put the block of feta in the middle.
3. Drizzle with the oil, sprinkle with chili flakes and pepper, and bake for 30 minutes until the tomatoes burst.
4. Meanwhile, cook the pasta in salted boiling water, saving a mug of the water before you drain it.
5. Mash the feta, garlic and tomatoes together into a creamy sauce.
6. Stir in the spinach, the pasta and a splash of pasta water until glossy.
7. Tear over the basil and serve.
