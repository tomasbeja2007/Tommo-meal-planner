## Recipe

- 480 g chickpeas, drained
- 150 g roasted red peppers, sliced
- 80 g black olives, halved
- 1 mozzarella, torn
- 4 slices sliced ham, torn
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 0.25 red onions, thinly sliced
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp dried oregano

1. Whisk the oil, vinegar and oregano.
2. Toss the chickpeas, peppers, olives, tomato and onion in the dressing and leave for 10 minutes.
3. Fold in the ham and mozzarella.
4. It keeps for 3 days in the fridge.
