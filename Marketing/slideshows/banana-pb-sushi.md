## Recipe

- 1 flour tortilla
- 2 tbsp peanut butter
- 1 banana
- 1 tsp chia seeds

1. Spread the tortilla with the peanut butter.
2. Lay the banana at one edge and roll it up tightly.
3. Slice into 8 rounds and sprinkle with chia seeds.
