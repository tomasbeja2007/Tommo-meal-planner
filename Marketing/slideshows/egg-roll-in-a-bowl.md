## Recipe

- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 500 g ground pork
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 500 g cabbage, shredded
- 2 carrots, grated
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sriracha
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 3 scallions, sliced
- 2 microwave rice pouches
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

1. Heat the oil in a large pan over a high heat and brown the pork for 6 minutes until crispy.
2. Add the garlic for 30 seconds.
3. Add the cabbage and carrot and stir-fry for 4 minutes until just soft but still crunchy.
4. Stir in the soy sauce.
5. Heat the rice and mix the sriracha with the mayonnaise.
6. Serve the pork and cabbage over rice with sriracha mayo, spring onions and sesame.
