## Recipe

- 300 g boneless chicken thighs, diced
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp hot sauce
- 1 tbsp butter
- 0.5 romaine lettuces, leaves separated
- 2 celery, finely sliced
- 1 carrot, grated
- 100 g plain Greek yogurt
- 0.5 tsp garlic powder
- 0.5 tsp dried thyme

1. Cook the chicken in the oil for 8 minutes until cooked through.
2. Add the hot sauce and butter and toss until glossy.
3. Mix the yogurt with the garlic powder, thyme and a pinch of salt for a quick ranch.
4. Spoon the chicken into lettuce cups with celery and carrot and drizzle with the ranch.
