## Recipe

- 480 g cannellini beans, drained
- 240 g chickpeas, drained
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 0.5 red onions, finely diced
- 100 g roasted red peppers, diced
- 100 g feta, crumbled
- 25 g parsley, chopped
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp red wine vinegar
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp salt

1. Whisk the oil, vinegar, mustard, oregano and salt in a large bowl.
2. Add the beans and chickpeas and toss to coat.
3. Stir in the cucumber, onion, peppers, feta and parsley.
4. Leave for 10 minutes before eating, or pack into jars for the week.
