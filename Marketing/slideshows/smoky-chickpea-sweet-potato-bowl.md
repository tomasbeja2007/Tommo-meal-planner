## Recipe

- 2 sweet potatoes, cubed
- 480 g chickpeas, drained
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 200 g quinoa
- 100 g kale, shredded
- 3 tbsp tahini
- 1 lemon
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp salt

1. Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
2. Toss the sweet potato, chickpeas and onion with the oil, paprika, cumin and salt on a large tray.
3. Roast for 30 minutes, shaking halfway, until the chickpeas are crisp.
4. Meanwhile, simmer the quinoa in 500 ml water for 15 minutes until the water is absorbed.
5. Whisk the tahini, lemon juice, garlic and 3 tbsp water into a pourable sauce.
6. Massage the kale with a pinch of salt for 1 minute to soften.
7. Fill bowls with quinoa, kale and the roast tray, then drizzle with tahini sauce.
