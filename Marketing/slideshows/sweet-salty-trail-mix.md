## Recipe

- 50 g roasted peanuts
- 40 g cashews
- 40 g raisins
- 30 g dark chocolate, chopped
- 20 g popcorn kernels

1. Pop the corn in a lidded pan or the microwave in a covered bowl.
2. Mix with the nuts, raisins and chocolate.
3. Split into 4 small tubs.
