## Recipe

- 80 g rolled oats
- 4 tbsp peanut butter
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp chia seeds
- 30 g dark chocolate, finely chopped
- 2 tbsp plain Greek yogurt

1. Mix everything together in a bowl.
2. Roll into 12 balls.
3. Chill for 30 minutes. They keep for a week in the fridge.
