## Recipe

- 60 g frozen edamame
- 1 avocado
- 0.5 limes
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 3 rice cakes
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 pinch red chili flakes

1. Boil the edamame for 3 minutes, then drain and cool.
2. Smash with the avocado, lime juice and soy sauce.
3. Spread over the rice cakes and finish with sesame and chili.
