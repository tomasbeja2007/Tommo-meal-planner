## Recipe

- 150 g basmati rice
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 145 g canned tuna, drained
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 2 tsp sriracha
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 0.5 cucumbers, diced
- 1 avocado, diced
- 2 scallions, sliced
- 1 nori sheets, crumbled

1. Cook the rice in salted boiling water for 12 minutes, then drain well.
2. Heat the oil in a non-stick pan over a medium-high heat and press the rice in a flat layer.
3. Leave it alone for 6 minutes until the bottom is golden and crisp, then break into pieces.
4. Mix the tuna with the mayonnaise, sriracha and half the soy sauce.
5. Pile the crispy rice into bowls with the cucumber and avocado.
6. Spoon over the spicy tuna, add the rest of the soy sauce and finish with spring onion and nori.
