## Recipe

- 3 chicken breasts, cut into bite-size pieces
- 100 g plain Greek yogurt
- 80 g panko breadcrumbs
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 250 g long-grain rice
- 200 g cabbage, shredded
- 1 lime
- 2 tbsp honey
- 1 tbsp sriracha
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 tsp salt

1. Coat the chicken in the yogurt and salt.
2. Mix the breadcrumbs with the paprika and garlic powder, then roll the chicken in them.
3. Air-fry at 200°C (400°F) for 12–15 minutes, shaking halfway, until golden and cooked through.
4. Meanwhile, cook the rice in salted boiling water for 12 minutes, then drain.
5. Toss the cabbage with the lime juice and mayonnaise.
6. Warm the honey and sriracha together and toss with the chicken.
7. Serve the chicken over rice with the slaw.
