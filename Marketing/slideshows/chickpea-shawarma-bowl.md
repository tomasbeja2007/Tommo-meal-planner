## Recipe

- 480 g chickpeas, drained
- 1 cauliflower, cut into small florets
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 0.5 tsp ground cinnamon
- 4 pita breads
- 3 tbsp tahini
- 1 lemon
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 15 g parsley, chopped
- 1 tsp salt

1. Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
2. Toss the chickpeas, cauliflower and onion with the oil, spices and salt on a large tray.
3. Roast for 25 minutes, shaking halfway, until charred at the edges.
4. Whisk the tahini with the lemon juice and 3 tbsp water until smooth.
5. Warm the pitas in the oven for the last 2 minutes and cut into strips.
6. Pile the tray into bowls with the cucumber and parsley.
7. Drizzle with tahini sauce and serve with the warm pita.
