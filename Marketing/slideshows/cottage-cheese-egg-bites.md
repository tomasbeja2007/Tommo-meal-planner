## Recipe

- 5 eggs
- 150 g cottage cheese
- 40 g spinach, chopped
- 20 g cheddar, grated
- 1 pinch salt
- 1 tsp olive oil

1. Heat the oven to 160°C (325°F) and oil 8 holes of a muffin tin.
2. Blend the eggs, cottage cheese and salt until silky.
3. Divide the spinach and cheddar between the holes, pour over the egg and bake for 22 minutes until just set.
4. Cool for 5 minutes before popping out. They keep 4 days in the fridge.
