## Recipe

- 2 mangoes
- 1 lime
- 0.5 tsp chili powder
- 1 pinch salt

1. Cut the mango into thick spears or cubes.
2. Squeeze over the lime juice.
3. Sprinkle with the chili powder and salt and eat straight away.
