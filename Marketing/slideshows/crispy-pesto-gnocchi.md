## Recipe

- 500 g gnocchi
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 150 g frozen peas
- 150 g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 3 tbsp basil pesto
- 20 g parmesan, grated
- 0.5 lemons
- 60 g spinach

1. Heat the oil in a large non-stick pan over a medium-high heat.
2. Add the gnocchi straight from the pack in one layer and leave for 4 minutes until golden underneath.
3. Toss and cook for 4 minutes more until crisp all over.
4. Add the peas, tomatoes and a splash of water and cook for 3 minutes.
5. Stir in the spinach until wilted, then the pesto and a squeeze of lemon.
6. Serve topped with the parmesan.
