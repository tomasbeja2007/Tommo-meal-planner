## Recipe

- 300 g grapes
- 1 lime
- 1 tsp brown sugar

1. Wash and dry the grapes, then pull them off the stalks.
2. Toss with the lime zest and juice and the sugar.
3. Spread on a tray and freeze for 2 hours until icy like sorbet.
