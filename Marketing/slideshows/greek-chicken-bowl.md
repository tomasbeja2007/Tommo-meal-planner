## Recipe

- 600 g boneless chicken thighs
- 1 lemon
- 2 tsp dried oregano
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 250 g couscous
- 1 tsp vegetable bouillon powder
- 200 g plain Greek yogurt
- 1 cucumber
- 250 g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 80 g feta, crumbled
- 1 tsp salt

1. Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
2. Toss the chicken with half the lemon juice, the oregano, two-thirds of the garlic, the oil and salt, and roast for 22–25 minutes until charred at the edges.
3. Pour 350 ml boiling water and the bouillon over the couscous, cover for 5 minutes, then fluff.
4. Grate half the cucumber, squeeze out the water and stir into the yogurt with the rest of the garlic and lemon. Dice the other half.
5. Slice the chicken and pile into bowls with couscous, cucumber, tomatoes and feta.
6. Spoon over the tzatziki.
