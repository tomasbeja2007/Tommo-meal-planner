## Recipe

- 250 g orzo
- 300 g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 1 mozzarella, torn
- 20 g basil
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 1 tsp salt

1. Cook the orzo in salted boiling water for 9 minutes, then rinse under cold water and drain.
2. Whisk the oil, balsamic, garlic and salt.
3. Toss the orzo with the tomatoes and dressing.
4. Fold through the mozzarella and torn basil just before eating.
