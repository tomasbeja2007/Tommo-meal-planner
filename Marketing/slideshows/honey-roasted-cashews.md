## Recipe

- 120 g cashews
- 1 tbsp honey
- 0.5 tsp red chili flakes
- 1 pinch salt

1. Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
2. Toss the cashews with the honey, chili and salt and spread on a lined tray.
3. Roast for 10 minutes, stirring once, then cool to crisp up.
