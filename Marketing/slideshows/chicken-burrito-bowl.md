## Recipe

- 250 g long-grain rice
- 2 limes
- 25 g cilantro, chopped
- 500 g boneless chicken thighs
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 240 g black beans, drained
- 165 g canned sweetcorn, drained
- 200 g tomato salsa
- 1 avocado, sliced
- 100 g sour cream
- 1 tsp salt

1. Cook the rice in salted boiling water for 12 minutes, drain and stir in the juice of 1 lime and half the cilantro.
2. Toss the chicken with the paprika, cumin, chili powder, oil and salt.
3. Cook in a frying pan over a medium-high heat for 6 minutes on each side until charred and cooked through.
4. Rest the chicken for 2 minutes, then slice.
5. Warm the black beans and sweetcorn in the same pan for 3 minutes.
6. Build the bowls: lime rice, beans and corn, chicken, salsa and avocado.
7. Top with sour cream, the rest of the cilantro and lime wedges.
