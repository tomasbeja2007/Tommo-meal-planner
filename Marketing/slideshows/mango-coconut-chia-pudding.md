## Recipe

- 4 tbsp chia seeds
- 200 ml coconut milk
- 150 ml soy milk
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 mango
- 0.5 limes

1. Whisk the chia seeds, coconut milk, soy milk and maple syrup and leave for 5 minutes.
2. Whisk again to break up any clumps, then chill for at least 4 hours.
3. Blend half the mango with the lime juice into a purée.
4. Layer the pudding with the mango purée and top with the rest of the mango, diced.
