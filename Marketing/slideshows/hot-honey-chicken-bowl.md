## Recipe

- 600 g boneless chicken thighs
- 2 sweet potatoes, cubed
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 3 tbsp honey
- 2 tsp hot sauce
- 0.5 tsp red chili flakes
- 1 broccoli, cut into florets
- 150 g plain Greek yogurt
- 1 lime
- 1 tsp salt

1. Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
2. Toss the chicken and sweet potato with the oil, paprika, garlic powder and salt on a large tray.
3. Roast for 15 minutes, then add the broccoli and roast for 15 minutes more.
4. Warm the honey with the hot sauce and chili flakes.
5. Brush the hot honey over the chicken for the last 3 minutes.
6. Mix the yogurt with the lime juice and a pinch of salt.
7. Slice the chicken and serve over the vegetables with the lime yogurt.
