## Recipe

- 160 g penne
- 300 g cherry tomatoes
- 120 g feta
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 0.25 tsp black pepper
- 8 g basil

1. Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
2. Put the cherry tomatoes in a small baking dish. Toss them with the olive oil, crushed garlic and black pepper.
3. Nestle the feta in the middle. Bake for 22 to 25 minutes, until the tomatoes burst and the feta is soft.
4. While the feta bakes, cook the penne according to the pack directions. Save a mug of pasta water before draining.
5. Mash the feta and tomatoes together in the baking dish. Stir in the penne and add pasta water a tablespoon at a time until the sauce coats it.
6. Tear the basil over the pasta and serve warm.
