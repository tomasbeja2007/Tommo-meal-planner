## Recipe

- 150 g long-grain rice
- 200 g raw shrimp
- 1 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 0.5 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 150 g cabbage, shredded
- 1 lime
- 100 g frozen sweetcorn
- 100 g tomato salsa
- 1 pinch salt

1. Cook the rice in salted boiling water for 12 minutes, adding the corn for the last 3, then drain.
2. Toss the shrimp with the spices and salt.
3. Cook in the oil over a high heat for 2 minutes on each side until pink and charred.
4. Toss the cabbage with half the lime juice.
5. Build the bowls with rice and corn, slaw, shrimp and salsa.
6. Squeeze over the rest of the lime.
