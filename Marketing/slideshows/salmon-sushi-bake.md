## Recipe

- 300 g basmati rice
- 3 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 salmon fillets
- 100 g cream cheese
- 3 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp sriracha
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 4 nori sheets
- 1 cucumber, cut into sticks
- 2 tsp sesame seeds

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Cook the rice for 12 minutes, drain and stir in the vinegar and sugar.
2. Bake the salmon on a lined tray for 12 minutes, then flake it.
3. Mix the salmon with the cream cheese, 2 tbsp mayonnaise and 1 tbsp soy sauce.
4. Press the rice into a baking dish, spread over the salmon and bake for 8 minutes until golden.
5. Drizzle with the rest of the mayonnaise mixed with the sriracha, the rest of the soy and sesame.
6. Scoop onto squares of nori with cucumber.
