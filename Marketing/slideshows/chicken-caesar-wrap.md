## Recipe

- 1 chicken breast, halved horizontally
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 0.5 romaine lettuces, shredded
- 25 g parmesan, grated
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 2 tbsp plain Greek yogurt
- 0.5 lemons
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 2 flour tortillas
- 1 pinch black pepper

1. Cook the chicken in the oil in a frying pan for 5 minutes on each side until cooked through, then slice.
2. Whisk the mayonnaise, yogurt, lemon juice, mustard, garlic, pepper and half the parmesan.
3. Toss the lettuce with the dressing and the rest of the parmesan.
4. Fill the tortillas with the salad and chicken and roll up tightly.
5. Toast seam-side down in the dry pan for 1 minute on each side, then cut in half.
