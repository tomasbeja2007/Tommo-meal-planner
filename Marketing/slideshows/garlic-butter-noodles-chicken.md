## Recipe

- 300 g spaghetti
- 500 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into strips
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 tbsp butter
- 8 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 30 g parmesan, grated
- 4 scallions, sliced
- 1 pinch salt

1. Cook the spaghetti in salted boiling water, saving a mug of water before draining.
2. Season the chicken with salt and fry in the oil over a high heat for 7 minutes until crisp and cooked, then set aside.
3. Turn the heat down, add the butter and garlic and cook gently for 2 minutes until golden and smelling amazing — don't let it burn.
4. Stir in the soy sauce and honey.
5. Toss in the spaghetti, parmesan and a splash of pasta water until glossy.
6. Top with the chicken and lots of spring onion.
