## Recipe

- 2 bananas
- 2 eggs
- 60 g rolled oats
- 1 tsp vegetable oil
- 100 g plain Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp maple syrup

1. Blend the bananas, eggs and oats until smooth and leave for 2 minutes to thicken.
2. Wipe a non-stick pan with a little oil over a medium heat.
3. Pour in small pancakes and cook for 2 minutes until bubbles appear, then flip for 1 minute.
4. Stack with yogurt and a drizzle of maple syrup.
