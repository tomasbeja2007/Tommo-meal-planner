## Recipe

- 600 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into strips
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp onion powder
- 1 tsp dried thyme
- 0.5 tsp chili powder
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 250 g long-grain rice
- 300 g frozen sweetcorn
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 150 g plain Greek yogurt
- 2 limes
- 2 scallions, sliced
- 1 tsp salt

1. Cook the rice in salted boiling water for 12 minutes, then drain.
2. Toss the chicken with the spices and salt.
3. Fry in the oil over a high heat for 8 minutes until blackened at the edges and cooked through, then set aside.
4. Add the corn and pepper to the hot pan and char for 5 minutes.
5. Mix the yogurt with the juice of 1 lime.
6. Serve the chicken over the rice with the charred corn, lime yogurt, spring onions and lime wedges.
