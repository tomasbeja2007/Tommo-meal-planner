## Recipe

- 480 g chickpeas, drained
- 0.5 red onions
- 2 cloves garlic
- 25 g parsley
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 200 g bulgur wheat
- 200 g hummus
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 1 lemon
- 1 tsp salt

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F).
2. Pulse the chickpeas, onion, garlic, most of the parsley, the spices, flour and salt in a blender until coarse.
3. Shape into 16 small patties and put on an oiled tray. Brush with the rest of the oil.
4. Bake for 25 minutes, turning halfway, until crisp outside.
5. Meanwhile, cover the bulgur with 400 ml boiling water and leave for 15 minutes, then fluff.
6. Toss the cucumber and tomato with the lemon juice and the rest of the parsley.
7. Spread hummus in each bowl, add the bulgur, salad and falafel.
