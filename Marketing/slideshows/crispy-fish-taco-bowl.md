## Recipe

- 3 cod fillets, cut into chunks
- 150 g plain Greek yogurt
- 60 g panko breadcrumbs
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 250 g long-grain rice
- 200 g red cabbage, shredded
- 2 limes
- 1 tsp hot sauce
- 1 tsp salt

1. Cook the rice in salted boiling water for 12 minutes, then drain.
2. Toss the fish in 3 tbsp of the yogurt, then in the breadcrumbs mixed with the paprika and salt.
3. Air-fry at 200°C (400°F) for 10 minutes until golden and flaky.
4. Toss the cabbage with the juice of 1 lime and a pinch of salt.
5. Stir the hot sauce into the rest of the yogurt.
6. Serve the fish over rice with the slaw, spicy yogurt and lime wedges.
