## Recipe

- 150 g basmati rice
- 1 salmon fillet
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp honey
- 100 g frozen edamame
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 2 tsp sriracha
- 0.5 cucumbers, sliced
- 1 nori sheets, cut into squares
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F). Cook the rice in salted boiling water for 12 minutes, adding the edamame for the last 3, then drain.
2. Brush the salmon with 1 tbsp soy sauce and the honey and bake on a lined tray for 12 minutes until it flakes.
3. Mix the mayonnaise and sriracha.
4. Flake the salmon into the hot rice and edamame with the rest of the soy sauce and mash it together, the way the viral version does — one fillet stretches to two bowls.
5. Top with cucumber, a zigzag of sriracha mayo and sesame seeds.
6. Scoop up each bite with a square of nori.
