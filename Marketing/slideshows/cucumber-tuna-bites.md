## Recipe

- 1 cucumber, thickly sliced
- 145 g canned tuna, drained
- 2 tbsp cream cheese
- 1 tsp sriracha
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

1. Mix the tuna with the cream cheese, sriracha and soy sauce.
2. Spoon a little onto each cucumber slice.
3. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and serve cold.
