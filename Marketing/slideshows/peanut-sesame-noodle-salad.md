## Recipe

- 200 g rice noodles
- 200 g red cabbage, shredded
- 2 carrots, cut into matchsticks
- 150 g frozen edamame
- 3 tbsp peanut butter
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 lime
- 30 g roasted peanuts, chopped

1. Soak the noodles in just-boiled water for 8 minutes, adding the edamame for the last 3, then rinse under cold water.
2. Whisk the peanut butter, soy sauce, vinegar, lime juice and 3 tbsp hot water.
3. Toss the noodles and edamame with the cabbage, carrot and dressing.
4. Scatter with peanuts and pack into jars for the week.
