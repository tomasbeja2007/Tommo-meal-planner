## Recipe

- 2 microwave rice pouches
- 150 g kimchi, chopped
- 1 tbsp toasted sesame oil
- 3 scallions, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 tbsp gochujang
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 2 eggs
- 1 nori sheets, crumbled
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

1. Lower the eggs into boiling water for 6½ minutes, then cool in cold water and peel.
2. Heat the sesame oil in a large frying pan over a high heat.
3. Fry the kimchi, garlic and most of the spring onion for 3 minutes.
4. Add the rice, gochujang and soy sauce and stir-fry for 4 minutes until the rice starts to crisp.
5. Spoon into bowls and halve the eggs on top.
6. Finish with nori, sesame seeds and the rest of the spring onion.
