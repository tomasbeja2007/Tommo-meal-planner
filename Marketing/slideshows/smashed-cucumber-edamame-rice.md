## Recipe

- 1 cucumber
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 clove garlic, crushed
- 0.5 tsp red chili flakes
- 1 tsp brown sugar
- 1 microwave rice pouch
- 150 g frozen edamame
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

1. Smash the cucumber with a rolling pin until it cracks, then break into chunks.
2. Toss with the salt and leave for 5 minutes, then drain off the water.
3. Whisk the soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil, garlic, chili flakes and sugar.
4. Microwave the rice and the edamame for 3 minutes, covered.
5. Toss the cucumber in the dressing and pile it over the edamame rice with the sesame seeds.
