## Recipe

- 200 g grapes
- 30 g dark chocolate, melted
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

1. Dip each grape halfway into the melted chocolate.
2. Sprinkle with sesame and lay on a lined tray.
3. Freeze for 1 hour until icy.
