## Recipe

- 2 mangoes, chopped
- 250 g plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp honey
- 0.5 limes
- 1 pinch ground cinnamon

1. Blend the mango, yogurt, honey, lime juice and cinnamon until smooth.
2. Pour into lolly moulds or small cups with sticks.
3. Freeze for at least 4 hours.
