## Recipe

- 200 g rolled oats
- 2 apples, grated
- 1 banana, mashed
- 1 egg
- 3 tbsp maple syrup
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 40 g raisins
- 1 tsp baking powder

1. Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and line a square tin.
2. Mix everything together.
3. Press into the tin.
4. Bake for 25 minutes until golden, then cool and cut into 8 bars.
