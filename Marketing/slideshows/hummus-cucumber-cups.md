## Recipe

- 1 cucumber, cut into thick rounds
- 100 g hummus
- 1 pinch smoked paprika
- 1 tsp olive oil

1. Scoop a little out of each cucumber round with a teaspoon to make cups.
2. Fill with hummus.
3. Finish with a drizzle of oil and a pinch of paprika.
