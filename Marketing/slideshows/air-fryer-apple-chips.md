## Recipe

- 2 apples, very thinly sliced
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp brown sugar

1. Toss the apple slices with the cinnamon and sugar.
2. Spread in a single layer in the air-fryer basket, in batches if needed.
3. Air-fry at 150°C (300°F) for 12–15 minutes, flipping halfway.
4. Leave to cool for 5 minutes. They crisp up as they cool.
