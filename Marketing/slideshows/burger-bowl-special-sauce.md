## Recipe

- 500 g ground beef
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1 romaine lettuce, shredded
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 0.5 cucumbers, diced
- 80 g cheddar, grated
- 3 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 tbsp ketchup
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 3 potatoes, cubed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp salt

1. Boil the potato cubes for 8 minutes until just tender, then drain.
2. Fry them in the oil in a large pan over a high heat for 6 minutes until crisp, then set aside.
3. Brown the beef and onion in the same pan for 8 minutes, seasoning with the garlic powder and salt.
4. Stir the mayonnaise, ketchup, mustard and a splash of the cucumber juice into a special sauce.
5. Fill the bowls with lettuce, tomato and cucumber.
6. Add the beef, crispy potatoes and cheddar, then drizzle over the sauce.
