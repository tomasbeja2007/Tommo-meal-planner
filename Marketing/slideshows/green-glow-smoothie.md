## Recipe

- 60 g spinach
- 2 bananas
- 1 mango, chopped
- 150 g plain Greek yogurt
- 200 ml milk

1. Blend the spinach with the milk first until no leaves are left.
2. Add the banana, mango and yogurt.
3. Blend until smooth.
4. Pour into two glasses.
