## Recipe

- 200 g cherry tomatoes
- 1 mozzarella, cut into cubes
- 10 g basil
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 pinch salt

1. Thread the tomatoes, mozzarella and basil onto small skewers.
2. Drizzle with the oil and balsamic.
3. Sprinkle with salt and serve.
