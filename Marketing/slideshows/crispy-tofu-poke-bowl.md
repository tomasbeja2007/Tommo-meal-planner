## Recipe

- 400 g firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 3 tbsp soy sauce
- 150 g basmati rice
- 120 g frozen edamame
- 1 carrot, grated
- 0.5 cucumbers, diced
- 1 mango, diced
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 1 tsp sriracha
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

1. Toss the tofu with 1 tbsp soy sauce, then with the cornstarch.
2. Air-fry at 200°C (400°F) for 15 minutes, shaking halfway, until crisp.
3. Meanwhile, cook the rice in salted boiling water for 12 minutes, adding the edamame for the last 3 minutes, then drain.
4. Whisk the rest of the soy sauce with the vinegar, sesame oil and sriracha.
5. Arrange the rice, edamame, carrot, cucumber and mango in bowls.
6. Add the crispy tofu, drizzle with the dressing and scatter with sesame seeds.
