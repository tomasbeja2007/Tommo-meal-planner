## Recipe

- 1 cucumber, thickly sliced
- 60 g cream cheese
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 pinch garlic powder
- 1 pinch red chili flakes

1. Spread or pipe the cream cheese onto the cucumber slices.
2. Mix the sesame, garlic powder and chili.
3. Sprinkle over and serve cold.
