## Recipe

- 60 g feta, crumbled
- 4 eggs, beaten
- 2 flour tortillas
- 40 g spinach
- 1 pinch red chili flakes
- 0.5 avocados, sliced
- 1 tsp hot sauce

1. Scatter half the feta in a non-stick pan over a medium heat and pour over half the egg.
2. When the egg is almost set, press a tortilla on top and cook for 1 minute.
3. Flip and cook for 1 minute more until the feta is crisp.
4. Fill with spinach, avocado, chili flakes and hot sauce, fold and repeat.
