## Recipe

- 250 g penne
- 290 g canned tuna, drained
- 200 g plain Greek yogurt
- 1 lemon
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 165 g canned sweetcorn, drained
- 200 g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 0.5 cucumbers, diced
- 2 scallions, sliced
- 1 tsp salt

1. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water until just tender, then rinse under cold water and drain.
2. Mix the yogurt, lemon juice, mustard and salt into a dressing.
3. Flake in the tuna and stir.
4. Add the pasta, sweetcorn, tomatoes, cucumber and spring onion and toss.
5. Split into containers and chill until lunch.
