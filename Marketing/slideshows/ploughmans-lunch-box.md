## Recipe

- 80 g cheddar, cut into chunks
- 2 slices sliced ham
- 1 apple, sliced
- 2 eggs
- 0.5 baguettes
- 1 celery, cut into sticks
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 80 g grapes

1. Boil the eggs for 8 minutes, then cool, peel and halve.
2. Slice the bread.
3. Pack the cheddar, ham, apple, eggs, celery and grapes into boxes.
4. Add a dab of mustard and the bread.
