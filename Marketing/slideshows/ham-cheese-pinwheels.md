## Recipe

- 2 flour tortillas
- 80 g cream cheese
- 4 slices sliced ham
- 30 g spinach
- 40 g cheddar, grated

1. Spread the tortillas with cream cheese.
2. Cover with ham, spinach and cheddar and roll up tightly.
3. Chill for 10 minutes, then slice into 1 cm pinwheels.
