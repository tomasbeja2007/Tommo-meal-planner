## Recipe

- 250 g frozen edamame
- 1 tsp toasted sesame oil
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 0.5 tsp red chili flakes
- 1 tsp soy sauce
- 1 pinch salt

1. Boil the edamame for 4 minutes, then drain well.
2. Warm the sesame oil in a pan and fry the garlic and chili for 30 seconds.
3. Toss in the edamame with the soy sauce and salt and serve warm.
