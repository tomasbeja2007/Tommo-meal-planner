## Recipe

- 1000 g gnocchi
- 2 bell peppers, chopped
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 250 g cherry tomatoes
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 mozzarella, torn
- 3 tbsp basil pesto
- 1 tsp salt

1. Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
2. Tip the gnocchi straight from the pack onto a large tray with the peppers, onion and tomatoes.
3. Toss with the oil, herbs and salt and spread out in one layer.
4. Roast for 20 minutes, shaking halfway, until the gnocchi are crisp.
5. Dot with the mozzarella and roast for 5 more minutes until melted.
6. Drizzle with pesto and serve straight from the tray.
