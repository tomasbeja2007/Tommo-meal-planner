## Recipe

- 100 g rolled oats
- 250 ml milk
- 100 g plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp peanut butter
- 20 g dark chocolate, finely grated
- 1 banana, sliced

1. Stir the oats, milk, yogurt and peanut butter together.
2. Stir in most of the grated chocolate and half the banana, mashed.
3. Split between two jars, cover and chill overnight.
4. Top with the rest of the banana and chocolate.
