## Recipe

- 2 bananas, halved
- 60 g dark chocolate, melted
- 20 g roasted peanuts, finely chopped

1. Push a lolly stick into each banana half.
2. Dip in the melted chocolate and roll in the peanuts.
3. Freeze on a lined tray for 2 hours.
