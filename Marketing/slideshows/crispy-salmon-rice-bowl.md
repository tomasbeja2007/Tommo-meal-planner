## Recipe

- 260 g salmon fillet, skin removed
- 120 g long-grain rice
- 0.5 cucumbers
- 1 avocado
- 2 scallions
- 2 tsp vegetable oil
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- 20 g mayonnaise
- 2 tsp sriracha

1. Cook the rice according to the pack directions. Keep it covered while you prepare the toppings.
2. Slice the cucumber and avocado, and thinly slice the scallions.
3. Mix the mayonnaise and sriracha in a small bowl.
4. Pat the salmon dry and cut it into bite-size cubes. Heat the vegetable oil in a skillet over medium-high heat.
5. Add the salmon in a single layer. Sear, turning to brown the sides, until the thickest piece reaches 63°C (145°F). Toss with the soy sauce off the heat.
6. Divide the rice between bowls. Add the salmon, cucumber and avocado. Drizzle with the sauce and scatter with scallions.
