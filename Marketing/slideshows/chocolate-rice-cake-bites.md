## Recipe

- 3 rice cakes, broken into pieces
- 40 g dark chocolate
- 1 tbsp peanut butter

1. Melt the chocolate with the peanut butter in the microwave.
2. Dip the rice cake pieces and lay on a lined tray.
3. Chill for 15 minutes until set.
