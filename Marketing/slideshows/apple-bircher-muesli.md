## Recipe

- 100 g rolled oats
- 1 apple, grated
- 200 ml milk
- 100 g plain Greek yogurt
- 20 g raisins
- 1 tsp honey

1. Mix the oats, grated apple, milk, yogurt and raisins.
2. Cover and chill overnight.
3. Stir and loosen with a splash of milk.
4. Drizzle with honey.
