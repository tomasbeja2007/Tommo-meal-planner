## Recipe

- 240 g ground beef
- 4 flour tortillas, small
- 60 g cheddar, thinly sliced
- 60 g romaine lettuce, shredded
- 25 g mayonnaise
- 15 g ketchup
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 0.25 tsp salt
- 0.25 tsp black pepper

1. Mix the mayonnaise, ketchup and mustard to make the burger sauce. Shred the lettuce.
2. Divide the beef into four portions. Press each portion into a thin, even layer on one side of a tortilla.
3. Season the beef with the salt and black pepper. Heat a heavy skillet over medium-high heat.
4. Cook the tortillas in batches, meat-side down, pressing briefly with a spatula. Cook until the beef is browned and reaches 71°C (160°F).
5. Flip each taco, add the cheddar and cook briefly to melt the cheese and toast the tortilla.
6. Top with shredded lettuce and burger sauce. Fold and serve warm.
