## Recipe

- 0.75 baguettes
- 2 slices sliced ham
- 0.5 mozzarella
- 0.25 romaine lettuces
- 1 tomato
- 0.25 red onions
- 2 tbsp mayonnaise
- 1 tsp red wine vinegar
- 0.5 tsp dried oregano
- 1 pinch black pepper

1. Pile the ham, mozzarella, lettuce, tomato and onion on a big board.
2. Chop everything together until it's finely mixed.
3. Add the mayonnaise, vinegar, oregano and pepper and chop again to mix.
4. Split the baguette, pile in the filling and cut in half.
