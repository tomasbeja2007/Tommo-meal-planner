## Recipe

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 400 g tomato sauce (passata)
- 480 g black beans, drained
- 165 g canned sweetcorn, drained
- 8 corn tortillas, torn
- 120 g cheddar, grated
- 1 avocado, diced
- 15 g cilantro, chopped
- 1 tsp salt

1. Heat the oil in a large lidded pan and cook the onion and pepper for 5 minutes.
2. Stir in the cumin and chili powder for 30 seconds.
3. Add the passata, beans, sweetcorn and salt and simmer for 5 minutes.
4. Stir in the torn tortillas until coated.
5. Scatter over the cheese, cover and cook for 4 minutes until melted.
6. Top with avocado and cilantro and serve from the pan.
