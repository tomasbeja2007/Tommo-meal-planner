## Recipe

- 4 rice cakes
- 2 tbsp peanut butter
- 1 banana, sliced
- 1 pinch ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp honey

1. Spread the rice cakes with peanut butter.
2. Top with banana slices.
3. Finish with cinnamon and a drizzle of honey.
