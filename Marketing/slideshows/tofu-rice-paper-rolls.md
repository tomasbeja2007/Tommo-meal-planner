## Recipe

- 8 rice paper wrappers
- 200 g firm tofu, cut into sticks
- 1 tsp vegetable oil
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 50 g rice noodles
- 1 carrot, cut into matchsticks
- 0.5 cucumbers, cut into matchsticks
- 2 tbsp peanut butter
- 0.5 limes

1. Fry the tofu in the oil for 6 minutes until golden, adding 1 tbsp soy sauce at the end.
2. Soak the noodles in just-boiled water for 5 minutes, then drain and cool.
3. Dip a rice paper wrapper in warm water for 10 seconds, fill with noodles, tofu, carrot and cucumber, fold in the sides and roll up tightly. Repeat.
4. Whisk the peanut butter with the rest of the soy sauce, the lime juice and 2 tbsp hot water to dip.
