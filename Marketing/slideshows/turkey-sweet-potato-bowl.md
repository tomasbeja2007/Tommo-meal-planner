## Recipe

- 2 sweet potatoes, cubed
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 500 g ground turkey
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 tsp chili powder
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 400 g diced tomatoes
- 240 g black beans, drained
- 100 g spinach
- 150 g plain Greek yogurt
- 1 lime
- 1 tsp salt

1. Heat the oven to 220°C (425°F).
2. Toss the sweet potato with the oil and a pinch of salt and roast for 28 minutes until golden.
3. Meanwhile, brown the turkey and onion in a large pan for 8 minutes.
4. Add the chili powder and cumin and cook for 1 minute.
5. Stir in the tomatoes and beans and simmer for 10 minutes. Season with salt.
6. Mix the yogurt with half the lime juice.
7. Serve the spinach topped with sweet potato, turkey chili and the lime yogurt.
