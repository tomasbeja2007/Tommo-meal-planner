## Recipe

- 3 chicken breasts, halved horizontally
- 2 tsp Italian seasoning
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 150 g roasted red peppers, sliced
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 250 g orzo
- 750 ml chicken stock
- 100 ml heavy cream
- 30 g parmesan, grated
- 100 g spinach
- 1 tsp salt

1. Season the chicken with the herbs and salt.
2. Heat the oil in a deep pan and cook the chicken for 4 minutes on each side until golden, then set aside.
3. Add the garlic, peppers and tomato paste and cook for 1 minute.
4. Stir in the orzo and stock and simmer for 10 minutes, stirring often.
5. Stir in the cream and parmesan, then nestle the chicken back in for 3 minutes until cooked through.
6. Stir through the spinach until wilted and serve.
