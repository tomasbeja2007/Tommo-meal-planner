## Recipe

- 400 g plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp honey
- 150 g frozen mixed berries
- 30 g dark chocolate, melted

1. Mix the yogurt with the honey and spread on a lined tray about 1 cm thick.
2. Scatter over the berries and drizzle with the melted chocolate.
3. Freeze for at least 3 hours.
4. Snap into shards and eat straight from the freezer.
