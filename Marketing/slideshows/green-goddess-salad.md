## Recipe

- 250 g cabbage, finely chopped
- 1 cucumber, finely chopped
- 3 scallions, sliced
- 40 g spinach
- 15 g basil
- 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 clove garlic
- 20 g parmesan
- 2 pita breads, cut into triangles
- 1 pinch salt

1. Heat the oven to 200°C (400°F) and bake the pita triangles for 8 minutes until crisp.
2. Blend the spinach, basil, lemon juice, oil, garlic, parmesan, salt and 3 tbsp water until smooth.
3. Toss the chopped cabbage, cucumber and spring onions in the green dressing.
4. Scoop it up with the pita chips.
