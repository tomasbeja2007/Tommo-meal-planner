## Recipe

- 125 g halloumi, sliced
- 100 g couscous
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 0.5 cucumbers, diced
- 40 g spinach
- 1 lemon
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp honey

1. Cover the couscous with 150 ml boiling water and a pinch of salt for 5 minutes, then fluff.
2. Fry the halloumi in a dry non-stick pan for 2 minutes on each side until golden.
3. Whisk the lemon juice, oil and honey and toss with the couscous, tomatoes, cucumber and spinach.
4. Top with the hot halloumi.
