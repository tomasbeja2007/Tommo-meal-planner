## Recipe

- 200 g quinoa
- 480 g chickpeas, drained
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 250 g cherry tomatoes, halved
- 0.5 red onions, finely sliced
- 100 g feta, crumbled
- 1 lemon
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp salt

1. Simmer the quinoa in 500 ml water for 15 minutes until absorbed, then spread out to cool.
2. Whisk the lemon juice, oil, oregano and salt.
3. Toss the quinoa, chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes and onion in the dressing.
4. Top with feta.
