## Recipe

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, chopped
- 2 cloves garlic, crushed
- 450 g roasted red peppers, drained
- 400 g diced tomatoes
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- 100 g feta, crumbled
- 4 slices sourdough loaf
- 1 tsp salt

1. Heat the oil in a pan and cook the onion for 6 minutes until soft.
2. Add the garlic and paprika for 1 minute.
3. Add the peppers, tomatoes and stock and simmer for 12 minutes.
4. Blend until smooth and season with salt.
5. Toast the sourdough and pile the crumbled feta on top.
6. Serve the soup with the feta toast for dunking.
