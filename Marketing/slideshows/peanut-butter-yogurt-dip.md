## Recipe

- 150 g plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp peanut butter
- 1 tsp honey
- 1 pinch ground cinnamon
- 2 apples, sliced

1. Whisk the yogurt, peanut butter, honey and cinnamon until smooth.
2. Serve with the apple slices for dipping.
