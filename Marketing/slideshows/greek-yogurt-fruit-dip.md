## Recipe

- 250 g plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp honey
- 0.5 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tbsp peanut butter
- 2 apples, sliced
- 200 g grapes
- 1 banana, sliced

1. Whisk the yogurt, honey, cinnamon and peanut butter.
2. Arrange the fruit around the dip.
3. Dip and eat straight away.
