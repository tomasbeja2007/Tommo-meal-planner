## Recipe

- 2 slices sourdough loaf
- 150 g cottage cheese
- 1 tomato, sliced
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 1 tsp sesame seeds
- 1 pinch garlic powder
- 1 pinch red chili flakes
- 1 pinch black pepper

1. Toast the sourdough.
2. Spread thickly with the cottage cheese.
3. Top with the tomato slices.
4. Mix the sesame seeds, garlic powder and chili and sprinkle over with the oil and pepper.
