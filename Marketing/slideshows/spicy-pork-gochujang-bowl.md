## Recipe

- 1 pork tenderloin, very thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp gochujang
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp honey
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 1 onion, sliced
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 250 g long-grain rice
- 1 cucumber, sliced
- 3 scallions, sliced
- 1 tsp sesame seeds

1. Cook the rice in salted boiling water for 12 minutes, then drain.
2. Mix the gochujang, soy sauce, honey and garlic and toss with the pork.
3. Heat the oil in a large pan until very hot and fry the onion for 2 minutes.
4. Add the pork in one layer and leave for 2 minutes to char, then stir-fry for 3 minutes until sticky and cooked.
5. Spoon over the rice with the cucumber.
6. Finish with spring onions and sesame seeds.
