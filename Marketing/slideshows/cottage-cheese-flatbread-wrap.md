## Recipe

- 250 g cottage cheese
- 2 eggs
- 0.5 tsp garlic powder
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- 3 slices sliced ham
- 0.25 romaine lettuces, shredded
- 1 tomato, sliced
- 1 pinch salt

1. Heat the oven to 180°C (350°F) and line a tray with baking paper.
2. Whisk the cottage cheese, eggs, garlic powder, herbs and salt until smooth, then spread into a thin rectangle.
3. Bake for 30 minutes until set and golden at the edges, then cool for 5 minutes and cut in half.
4. Fill each half with ham, lettuce and tomato and roll up.
