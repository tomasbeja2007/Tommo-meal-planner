## Recipe

- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 2 salmon fillets
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 250 g orzo
- 1 tsp vegetable bouillon powder
- 1 lemon
- 200 g frozen peas
- 80 g spinach
- 20 g parmesan, grated
- 1 pinch salt

1. Season the salmon and cook in the oil, skin-side down, for 4 minutes in a deep lidded pan, then lift out.
2. Add the garlic and orzo and stir for 1 minute.
3. Pour in 750 ml boiling water and the bouillon and simmer for 8 minutes, stirring now and then.
4. Stir in the peas, spinach and the lemon zest and juice.
5. Sit the salmon on top, cover and cook for 4 minutes, then break it into big flakes through the orzo.
6. Finish with parmesan.
