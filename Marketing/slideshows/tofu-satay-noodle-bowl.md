## Recipe

- 400 g firm tofu, pressed and cubed
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 1 tbsp vegetable oil
- 150 g rice noodles
- 3 tbsp peanut butter
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 lime
- 1 tbsp maple syrup
- 2 bok choy, halved
- 100 g bean sprouts
- 2 scallions, sliced

1. Toss the tofu in the cornstarch.
2. Fry in the oil in a non-stick pan for 10 minutes, turning, until crisp all over.
3. Meanwhile, soak the noodles in just-boiled water for 8 minutes, then drain.
4. Whisk the peanut butter, soy sauce, lime juice, maple syrup and 4 tbsp hot water into a satay sauce.
5. Add the bok choy to the pan with a splash of water for 2 minutes.
6. Toss the noodles with half the sauce and top with tofu, bok choy, bean sprouts and spring onions, then drizzle with the rest.
