## Recipe

- 400 g udon noodles
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 3 scallions, sliced
- 1 tsp red chili flakes
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp rice vinegar
- 2 tsp sesame seeds

1. Cook the udon according to the pack directions, then drain well.
2. Mince the garlic and slice the scallions. Keep the green tops for serving.
3. Dry the pan, add the vegetable oil and warm it over medium heat.
4. Add the garlic and pale scallion slices. Stir for about 30 seconds, until fragrant. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the chili flakes and sesame seeds.
5. Add the udon, soy sauce and rice vinegar to the pan. Toss until the noodles are glossy and well coated.
6. Divide between bowls and scatter with the sliced scallion tops.
