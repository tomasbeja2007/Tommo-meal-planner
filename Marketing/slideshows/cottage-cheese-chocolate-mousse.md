## Recipe

- 200 g cottage cheese
- 30 g dark chocolate
- 1 tbsp honey

1. Melt the chocolate in the microwave in 20-second bursts.
2. Blend the cottage cheese, melted chocolate and honey until completely smooth.
3. Spoon into two glasses and chill for 10 minutes.
