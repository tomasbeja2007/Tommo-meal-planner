## Recipe

- 350 g penne
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 0.5 onions, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, crushed
- 4 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp red chili flakes
- 150 ml heavy cream
- 40 g parmesan, grated
- 15 g basil
- 1 pinch black pepper

1. Cook the pasta in salted boiling water, saving a mug of the water before you drain it.
2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a large pan and cook the onion for 4 minutes.
3. Add the garlic, tomato paste and chili flakes and fry for 3 minutes until brick red.
4. Stir in the cream and simmer for 2 minutes.
5. Add the pasta, parmesan and a splash of pasta water and toss until silky.
6. Season with pepper and tear over the basil.
