Subscription

How do I cancel Tommo Pro or my free trial?

On your iPhone open Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Tommo and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge. Check the date shown by Apple. Deleting the app or your data in Tommo does not cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Subscriptions are sold by Apple, so refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose "Request a refund". We can't issue App Store refunds ourselves, but we're happy to help if something went wrong.

I paid but Tommo still shows the paywall

Tap Restore on the paywall, or go to Profile → Restore purchases, making sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you used to buy. If it still doesn't work, email us with the date of purchase (no card details needed).

Can my family use my subscription?

If Family Sharing is available for Tommo Pro in your country and switched on for your subscription, family members can use it on their own devices.

Your plan and recipes

Are the prices exact?

No, they're estimates for the type of shop and region you picked, to help you plan within a budget. Real shop prices and offers vary.

Can I trust the allergy filter?

It removes recipes containing the allergens you selected, based on our ingredient data, but it is not a guarantee. Always read the labels of what you buy: brands and "may contain" warnings vary.

How do I change my answers, budget or shop?

Go to Profile. Every answer from the first set-up can be changed there; regenerate your week to use the new settings.

How do calorie targets work?

If you set a daily target, Tommo picks recipes that suit it and sizes each day's portions so one adult's day lands near it. The numbers are estimates, not medical advice.

iCloud and devices

My plan isn't syncing to my other device

Check that both devices use the same Apple Account, that iCloud is on, and that Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Apps using iCloud → Tommo is enabled. Sync can take a few minutes. Low Power Mode and low iCloud storage can delay it.

Do I need an account?

No. Tommo works without signing in. Sign in with Apple is optional.

Your data

What do you know about me?

Almost nothing. Your answers, plans and recipes stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud, which we can't see. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

In the app: Profile → Delete account and all data. This deletes the app’s data from this device. If iCloud sync is active, the deletions sync to iCloud. If sync is unavailable, check your iCloud storage settings. To stop using Sign in with Apple for Tommo, open your Apple Account, then go to Sign-In and Security → Sign in with Apple → Tommo. To exercise any other data protection right, email tomasbeja2007@icloud.com.
