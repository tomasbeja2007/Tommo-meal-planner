2. What this policy covers

This policy explains what personal data is involved when you use the Tommo app ("the app") and this website, why, on what legal basis, who else is involved, how long it is kept, and your rights under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR and other applicable data protection laws.

3. Data that stays with you (we never receive it)

The app is designed so that the information you give it is processed on your device. When iCloud is switched on for the app, Apple stores a copy in your private iCloud database so it can sync between your devices. That database belongs to your Apple Account; we, as the app's developer, have no access to its contents.

This includes:

  • Your answers and preferences: the name you choose to give, your goals and obstacles, household size, cooking days, meal types, weekly budget, country, chosen supermarkets, kitchen appliances, cuisines and "vibes" you like, ingredients you don't eat, the reminder times you set, and how you heard about the app.
  • Diet, allergies and calorie target: these can reveal information about your health and are treated as special category data under Article 9 GDPR. You choose whether to enter them; they are used only on your device to filter recipes and size portions, and they are never sent to us.
  • Your plans and activity: weekly meal plans, grocery lists and ticks, recipes you save, rate, cook or mark as "not for me", your own recipes, and any personal photos you add to a recipe (only the photo you pick in Apple's photo picker; the app has no access to the rest of your library).
  • Sign in with Apple (optional): if you use it, Apple gives the app a unique identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and an email address (which can be Apple's private relay address). These are stored on your device and in your private iCloud only, to remember that you signed in. We do not receive them.

Because we do not receive or store this data, we cannot access, change or delete it on your behalf. You stay in control (see section 9). Apple's handling of iCloud and Sign in with Apple is described in Apple's Privacy Policy; Apple acts as an independent controller for those services.

5. Things we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad identifiers (IDFA), no tracking across apps or websites, no data brokers.
  • No analytics or marketing SDKs and no social-media SDKs in the app.
  • No selling or renting of personal data, and no "sharing" for advertising purposes.
  • No location access: the app uses your iPhone's region setting only to suggest currency and supermarkets.
  • No cookies or tracking on this website.

6. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules its reminders (for example your weekly planning reminder and a reminder before a free trial ends) locally on your iPhone. No push server of ours is involved. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

9. Your rights and how to use them

Under the GDPR and the UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • rectify inaccurate data;
  • erase data ("right to be forgotten");
  • restrict processing;
  • data portability, where processing is based on consent or contract and carried out by automated means;
  • object at any time to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

For data in the app: because it lives on your devices and in your iCloud, you exercise these rights directly. You can change any answer in Profile, and Profile → Delete all my data erases everything the app has stored (on this device and, when iCloud is on, in your iCloud copy). Deleting the app removes the local copy; you can also delete the app's iCloud data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

For data we hold (support emails, records from Apple): email [PRIVACY EMAIL]. We reply within one month, free of charge, and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU country where you live or work, or where you believe the infringement happened. 10. Children

Tommo is a general-audience cooking and planning app. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16 (or the lower age set by the law of their country). Subscriptions require an Apple Account that can make purchases. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

Data in the app is protected by iOS data protection and, when synced, by Apple's iCloud security (encrypted in transit and at rest). We keep support emails in an access-protected mailbox with two-factor authentication. No system is perfectly secure; if a personal data breach affects data we hold and is likely to put you at risk, we will notify the authority and, where required, you.
